Doylestown Dental had a great chairside reputation but a website that scared
people off — slow, dated, and impossible to book from on a phone.

## The challenge

New patients were finding the practice on Google, landing on a cluttered page,
and bouncing to a competitor with a cleaner site. The office was fielding
booking requests by phone only, during hours they were busy treating patients.

## What we did

We rebuilt the site around one job: **get the right new patient to book in under
a minute.** A calm, bright design signals "modern, gentle dentistry," and a
sticky "Book a visit" button follows you down every page into a simple
appointment flow.

- Clear service pages for the treatments people actually search for
- A one-screen booking form wired straight to the front desk
- Local SEO so "dentist in Doylestown" surfaces the practice first

## The result

Within three months, new-patient bookings were up **38%**, and the front desk
went from playing phone tag to confirming appointments that came in overnight.
