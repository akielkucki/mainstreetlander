Maple & Main makes the best breakfast on the block — but every order still came
through a crowded counter and a ringing phone.

## The challenge

The café wanted online ordering before a holiday rush, with no time for a
six-week build and no appetite for a 20%-a-ticket delivery app.

## What we did

We shipped a warm, appetizing site with **commission-free online ordering** wired
to the kitchen — in a weekend:

- A mouth-watering menu that doubles as the ordering system
- Card payments and pickup times handled on-site, no third-party cut
- A simple dashboard so staff can 86 an item in two taps

## The result

Online ordering went live **over a single weekend**, ahead of the rush — and the
café keeps the full ticket instead of handing a fifth of it to a delivery app.
