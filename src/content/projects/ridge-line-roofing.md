Roofing is a "right now" purchase — when a shingle blows off, the homeowner who
shows up first usually wins the job. Ridge Line was invisible at that moment.

## The challenge

The company relied on word of mouth and a one-page site that never ranked. After
a storm, the phones lit up for competitors who showed up at the top of Google,
not for Ridge Line.

## What we did

We built a fast, proof-heavy site and tuned it to own local search:

- Service-area pages targeting each town Ridge Line covers
- Before/after galleries and reviews front and center to build trust
- A two-tap quote request that works one-handed on a roof or a couch
- A fully optimized Google Business Profile feeding the same form

## The result

Ridge Line now ranks **#1 for "roofer near me"** across its core towns, and storm
weeks turn into a steady queue of quote requests instead of missed calls.
