Bucks County Bikes sells bikes, but its bread and butter is service — tune-ups,
repairs and fittings. The old site buried all of it.

## The challenge

Customers couldn't tell the shop did repairs at all, so service revenue depended
on walk-ins. Quote requests trickled in through a generic contact form nobody
trusted.

## What we did

We put service front and center and made asking for a quote effortless:

- A dedicated repair menu with transparent starting prices
- A guided quote flow ("what's the bike, what's wrong") that sets expectations
- Featured inventory to pull browsers in off local searches

## The result

Inbound quote requests jumped to **2.1× their previous volume**, smoothing out the
slow weekdays the shop used to dread.
