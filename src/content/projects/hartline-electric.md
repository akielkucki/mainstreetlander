Hartline Electric did clean, code-perfect work but looked like every other
electrician in the phone book — a clip-art bolt and a Gmail address.

## The challenge

Homeowners couldn't tell Hartline apart from the cheapest bid in the inbox, so
the company kept competing on price for work it should have won on trust.

## What we did

We rebuilt the brand from the ground up so it looks as sharp as the wiring:

- A confident wordmark and color system that reads "licensed and professional"
- Messaging that leads with safety, warranty and on-time arrival
- Branded trucks and uniforms that turn every job into a billboard
- A refreshed site carrying the new identity end to end

## The result

The premium look paid off where it counts: quote calls roughly **tripled**, and
Hartline stopped being the bid people use just to negotiate someone else down.
