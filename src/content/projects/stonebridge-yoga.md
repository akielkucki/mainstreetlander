Stonebridge had a loyal community but ran its schedule on a wall calendar and a
group text. Newcomers had no way in.

## The challenge

First-timers would visit the site, fail to find a class time they could book,
and never come back. Regulars held spots they didn't use, leaving teachers
guessing how full each class would be.

## What we did

We designed a calm, breathing site with booking built into the schedule:

- A live class timetable you can reserve from in two taps
- Memberships and class packs sold and managed on-site
- Gentle reminders that cut no-shows and freed up waitlisted spots
- Local SEO so "yoga near me" finds the studio, not a big-box gym

## The result

Class bookings climbed **64%**, mats fill predictably, and teachers walk in
knowing exactly who's coming.
